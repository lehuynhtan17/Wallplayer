WALLPLAYER — your desktop wallpaper, playing (Windows 10 / 11)
================================================================

GETTING STARTED
1. Unzip the whole "Wallplayer" folder and keep its files together. Put it somewhere you can write to,
   such as Documents (not Program Files), so Wallplayer can update itself.
2. Double-click Wallplayer.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" → "Run anyway".
3. Wallplayer fills the screen behind every window, like a wallpaper. Its icon is in the tray by the clock.
4. Move the mouse to the bottom edge of the screen to bring up the taskbar (an auto-hide taskbar too).
5. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space from anywhere to search for music.
Wallplayer needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 11 and most Windows 10 PCs already have.

THE SCREEN
- Centre, top to bottom: the clock → the cover flow (the song playing in the middle, the next ones on each
  side) → the search field → the player bar.
- Left: the playlist ("Continue Playing").
- Scroll or drag over the cover flow to browse it; click a card to play it.
- Rows that are wider than the panel (Top Results, colour swatches, the cover flow) scroll with the mouse
  wheel, a sideways swipe on a touchpad, or by dragging.
- Hidden parts (H, the collapse button, Full lyrics, Cover flow / Playlist turned off) no longer take clicks,
  so you cannot change song by clicking an invisible card.

THE PLAYER BAR (left to right)
Platform · Previous · Play / Pause · Next · Repeat (off → whole list → this song)
· Cover and title (the progress line below it: click to jump) · ♡ Like
· 💬 Lyrics & subtitles · Cover flow (lit while the cover flow shows; click for the full-lyrics view)
· ≡ Playlist · ☾ Dim wallpaper
· 🔊 Volume: point at it for the slider, click to mute, scroll to change. Songs playing inside Wallplayer
  use the player volume; songs playing in Spotify or another app use the Windows volume.
· ··· Settings (see SETTINGS below).
In the search field: ✕ clears what you typed · ◎ music video wallpaper · the collapse button hides the
whole interface (H, or a double-click on the wallpaper, brings it back).

SEARCH
- Click the search field (or press Ctrl + Alt + Space): with nothing typed you see "Music for you",
  "Recently played" and "Recent playlists" (playlists and albums you played lately).
- "Music for you" (YouTube, signed in: Settings → Accounts): the playlists YouTube picks for you under
  Home → Music — your Mixes, My Mix, artist mixes — in the same rows as on YouTube. Click one to play
  it; a Mix keeps going like on YouTube and shows as "YouTube Mix" on the left. It reloads every 15
  minutes. Signed out, a small card offers to sign in (× hides it).
- Playlist cards from YouTube Music have a heart: it saves the playlist / album to your YouTube Music
  library (sign in to YouTube first: Settings → Accounts). The + button above Continue Playing saves
  the songs on the left as a new private playlist in YouTube Music.
- Typing shows suggestions (click one to search it) and results in four tabs: All / Songs / Singers /
  Playlists (playlists and albums from every platform). ↑ ↓ to choose, Enter to play, Esc to close.
- Click a singer to play their best songs; click a playlist or album to play all of it.
- Point at a song: ▶≡ "Play next" plays it right after the current song, ≡+ "Add to playlist" adds it at
  the end. The panel stays open so you can add several songs.
- When the playlist runs out, Wallplayer adds similar songs (YouTube Music / SoundCloud radio) so the music
  keeps going. Turn it on or off in Settings → Now Playing → "Keep playing similar songs".

PLATFORMS (search goes to the platform you choose)
- YouTube Music: songs play inside the wallpaper, with the music video behind them.
- YouTube: videos and clips; the clip itself plays full-screen as the wallpaper.
- SoundCloud: plays inside the wallpaper.
- Spotify: searches Spotify and plays in the Spotify app (needs your account and Premium).
- Spotify Web Player / Apple Music / Amazon Music: opens the song in your browser or app.
A song you start from Wallplayer in Spotify, Spotify Web or Apple Music comes back to the player through
Windows (cover, lyrics, colours, controls), and so do Spotify's next songs after it.

ACCOUNTS (platform icon → Accounts…, or Settings → Accounts)
- YouTube / YouTube Music: sign in with Google; with Premium there are no ads.
- SoundCloud: sign in (Go+ plays full tracks).
- Spotify: create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard, add the Redirect URI
  http://127.0.0.1:43821/callback, paste its Client ID and click Connect.

THE PLAYLIST (left)
- Click ONE YouTube song (in the search results or in History Playing): it plays right away and the
  playlist becomes "YouTube Mix" — the Mix YouTube makes for that song (personal when you are signed in to
  YouTube). It replaces Continue Playing and keeps going: near the end Wallplayer loads the next part.
- Prefer the old way? Settings → Now Playing → When you pick a song → "Mix + your picks": YouTube's
  suggestions for that song, mixed with picks from your account.
- ↻ makes a fresh Mix (or fresh suggestions) for the song playing. The clock button next to it switches to "History Playing": your last 100
  songs; click one to play it again, click the button again to go back.
- Point at a song: ▶≡ plays it next, ✕ removes it. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to change the order.
- Scroll with the mouse wheel or drag up and down.

YOUR OWN MUSIC (songs on this PC)
- Click the folder button next to the playlist title → "Add a folder…" and pick a music folder
  (mp3, m4a, flac, wav, ogg…). Or drop folders or songs onto Wallplayer while it is in front
  (Ctrl + Alt + Space, or with "Behind desktop icons" off).
- Titles, artists and covers are read from the files ("Artist - Title" file names work too). Lyrics and
  the edge light work as usual.
- Folders are remembered: next time click "Play my music" or "Shuffle". "Remove" forgets a folder.
- Drop a picture or a video onto Wallplayer to make it the wallpaper.

LYRICS & SUBTITLES (💬 button)
- Lyrics are found online and timed to the song (LRCLIB, NetEase); otherwise Genius (timing estimated).
  "Search Google for lyrics" opens your browser.
- Word by word: when NetEase has the timing of every word, the words light up exactly as they are sung,
  in every effect. Local .lrc lyrics with a time before each word work the same way.
- Line 1 / Line 2: choose the languages (Original, Tiếng Việt, English, 日本語, 한국어…). Lines without a
  translation are machine-translated, keeping their timing.
- YouTube clips: the video's own captions become the lyrics, in the player's style. Without captions,
  normal lyrics are used.
- Timing: "This song" moves the lyrics of this song only, "All songs" moves every song (+ shows the words
  earlier). Click the number to go back to 0. Wallplayer remembers it. The [ and ] keys also move this song.
- Other versions: pick another version, or type a title and artist and press Enter to search again.
- Long instrumental parts show a ♪ with three dots that fill up until the next line.
- Full lyrics (cover flow button or L): hides the cover flow and shows the lyrics large in the centre.
- Apple Music effect (Settings → Lyrics → Effect): the song as a column, like Apple Music. The line being
  sung is bright and fills in word by word, the others are dim and softly blurred, and the column glides
  up line by line; long pauses show three breathing dots. Translations sit under each line.
- Turn off "Find lyrics online" in Settings → Lyrics if you do not want lyrics looked up.

CLOCK & LYRICS EDITOR (like the iPhone lock screen)
- Click the clock (or press E, or Settings → Clock / Lyrics → "Font, colour & glass…").
- At the top choose Clock or Lyrics (or click the outlined clock or lyrics).
- Font: the "12" / "Aa" tiles. Weight: how bold the clock is. Size: lyrics size.
- Solid or Glass: solid letters, or glass letters that blur and brighten the wallpaper behind them.
- Colour: Auto (follows the album), colours taken from the cover, the iPhone palette, or "+" for any
  colour. The bar below makes it darker or lighter. Opacity; Glass: Clear → Frost → Milky.
- Done, Esc or a click outside closes it. Everything is saved.

SETTINGS (··· button at the end of the player bar)
A window like macOS System Settings: pages on the left (type in Search to find one), settings on the right.
Esc or a click outside closes it.
- Now Playing: open the song on its platform, Shuffle, Keep playing similar songs, Music video wallpaper,
  your own wallpaper image or video.
- Appearance: theme color taken from the album cover by how much of it each colour covers: Accent /
  Dominant / Secondary, or click a swatch. Used by the clock, the edge light and the accents. Dim wallpaper.
- Wallpaper:
  · Music video / cover: the music video of the song, kept in time; the album cover when there is none.
    Dots above the search field let you pick the song's own video or another video by the same artist.
    The video is kept in time with the sound. If the singing still doesn't match, that video is a different
    cut of the song (a longer intro, a story part, a radio edit): pick another dot, or play the song's
    YouTube video itself (then the picture and the sound come from the same video).
  · My video: search YouTube for any video; it plays muted and loops behind everything.
  · Surprise me: city, nature and first-person walk videos picked to fit your songs; "New video each
    song", or "Another one" to change now.
  · Desktop wallpaper: keeps your Windows wallpaper; the skin sits on top.
  Blur softens the background. The ◎ button turns the video background on and off.
  Display: which screen Wallplayer uses (or tray menu → "Move to next display"). Portrait screens get a
  vertical layout. Tray menu → "Fill other displays" shows the same skin on the other screens, without
  controls or sound; they follow the main screen's settings. Video quality (YouTube): Auto, 360p … 4K.
- Clock: on / off; 7 styles; position Top / Top left / Top right / Bottom left / Bottom right.
- Lyrics: Player style (karaoke) or YouTube captions; 14 effects (Stack, Apple Music, Line, Bounce,
  Typewriter, Glow, Pop, Karaoke fill, Shake, Jiggle, Glitch, Wave, Zoom, Slide); position Center / Left /
  Right / Bottom (on a portrait screen, Center / Left / Right put the lyrics right under the cover flow and
  Bottom puts them under the playlist); 5 fonts, 3 sizes, lower case / Title / UPPER CASE.
- Ambient Light (the edge light): on / off; colour from the album cover or the live video; 11 effects
  (Comet, Pulse, Spectrum, Rainbow, Aurora, Fire, Lava, Snake, Sparkle, Neon, Wave) — point at one to
  preview it; Where: whole frame, bottom, top or corners; Light on each display; Intensity and Blend.
- Layout: Player bar, Search field, Cover flow, Playlist, and "Album cover as the tray icon" (the tray icon
  shows the song's cover with a ring that beats).
- Performance: Power Auto (lighter on battery or with Energy Saver), Full or Saver (30 fps, video up to
  720p). "Rest behind full-screen and maximised windows": Wallplayer stops drawing and pauses the
  background video while a game, a film or a maximised window covers the screen; the music keeps
  playing. When the music stops, the light slows down and stops drawing. "Now: …" shows the mode.
  "High priority" (Startup): opens Wallplayer as soon as you sign in, before the other apps, and loads
  it at high priority (see "Start with Windows" below).
- Accounts: see ACCOUNTS above.
- General: Language (English or Tiếng Việt, also the tray menu; Wallplayer uses Vietnamese when Windows
  does), Behind desktop icons, Search from anywhere, Updates, Privacy policy, Self-test, Quit.

UPDATES (no installer)
- Each time it starts, Wallplayer quietly checks GitHub once. When there is a new version, the tray menu
  item becomes "Update available: vX.Y.Z".
- Click it (or "Check for updates" in the tray menu, or Settings → General → Updates) to read the release notes
  and choose Update or Skip. Update downloads it and opens the new version; Skip stops offering that one.
- Turn off the start-up check in Settings → General → "Check when Wallplayer starts".
- The new Wallplayer.exe replaces the old one in the same folder (the old file is kept as
  Wallplayer.exe.old until the next start).

TIKTOK
- Choose TikTok with the platform button (left of the player bar). The middle of the screen becomes one
  vertical video at a time, like the TikTok app: no playlist, the clock moves to the left, and the
  video's picture, blurred, lights the whole screen behind it.
- Next / previous video: scroll the mouse wheel over the video, press ↓ / ↑, or use the ▲ ▼ buttons
  (and ⏮ ⏭ on the player bar). Click the video to pause or play it.
- Auto scroll (the button under ▼): on, the next video starts when one ends; off, each video loops.
- Beside the video, like TikTok: ♥ Like, comments, Save (your TikTok Favorites) and Share (copies the
  link). Like and Save use your TikTok account, so sign in to TikTok first; comments open on the right
  (writing one opens the video on TikTok).
- "For You" is your TikTok feed when you are signed in to TikTok (Settings → Accounts → TikTok),
  TikTok's general feed when you are not. Type in the search field and press Enter to watch a search;
  the For You button brings the feed back. "Up next" on the right shows the next three videos;
  "See more" under a long caption shows all of it.
- How it works: TikTok has no public feed for apps, so Wallplayer opens TikTok's own page out of sight
  (with your TikTok session) to read the list of videos, and plays each video in TikTok's official embed
  player. If TikTok asks for a check, press "Open TikTok", answer it, then "Done".
- Other displays show the same video (as a still frame) and its details.
- Pick another platform to leave TikTok. Wallplayer starts again on TikTok if that is where you left it
  (paused until you press play).

MUSIC IN OTHER APPS
Wallplayer only shows what you play from Wallplayer. A video in a browser tab (Facebook, YouTube…), a game,
or music you start in another app by yourself is ignored: no cover, no lyrics, no music video, nothing in
History. Wallplayer never pauses other apps either: both just play.

SHORTCUT
One global shortcut: "Search from anywhere" (Ctrl + Alt + Space by default) opens the search from any app.
Change it in Settings → General → Search from anywhere: click the button, then press a new key combination (with
Ctrl, Alt or Shift, or F1–F24) or the middle / side mouse buttons (with Ctrl, Alt or Shift if you like).
Esc cancels, Reset goes back to Ctrl + Alt + Space. The left and right mouse buttons cannot be used.
When Wallplayer is in front: Space play / pause, ← → previous / next, ↑ ↓ volume, H hide the interface,
L full lyrics, E editor, [ ] lyrics timing, / search.

ON THE DESKTOP (tray icon, right-click)
- By default (like Wallpaper Engine) Wallplayer lives in the wallpaper layer, behind the desktop icons, so
  Win + D, Show desktop and Win + M keep it on screen. The mouse still works on empty desktop space:
  point, click, drag, scroll. Clicking the search field brings Wallplayer forward so you can type; a click
  outside sends it back. Single-key shortcuts work only while it is in front (Ctrl + Alt + Space).
- Untick "Behind desktop icons" to make it a normal window at the bottom: it covers the desktop icons and
  every button takes clicks directly; after Win + D it tries to come forward (less reliable).
- "Hide desktop icons" (on by default) hides every desktop icon, Recycle Bin included, while Wallplayer
  runs and gives them back when you quit. (If Wallplayer was ended from Task Manager and the icons are
  still hidden: start Wallplayer and untick it, or right-click the desktop → View → Show desktop icons.)
- "Start with Windows": starts Wallplayer when you sign in (also in Settings → General). It waits for the
  desktop to be ready before going behind the icons. If you move the Wallplayer folder, open Wallplayer once
  from the new place and it points Windows to it.
- "High priority" (Settings → Performance): Wallplayer opens as soon as you sign in, before the other
  startup apps (Windows normally starts them one by one, with a delay), and runs at high priority until
  the skin is up, then goes back to normal. It uses a Task Scheduler task of your own account
  ("Wallplayer - <your name>"), so no administrator rights are needed. Turn it off to go back to the
  normal start.

IF WALLPLAYER DOES NOT OPEN WHEN YOU SIGN IN
1. In Settings → General, turn "Start with Windows" off and on again, or turn on "High priority" in Settings → Performance. This also switches it back on in
   Windows' own list (Task Manager → Startup apps, or Settings → Apps → Startup), where it may be off.
2. Wallplayer is not code-signed yet, and files from a downloaded zip are marked as coming from the
   internet. Right-click Wallplayer.exe → Properties → tick "Unblock" → OK, so Windows does not hold it back.
3. Still nothing: %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.tan.wallplayer\wallplayer.log shows "started with Windows" when Windows
   did start it.

IF THE SKIN DISAPPEARS
Wallplayer runs its interface in Microsoft Edge WebView2. On Windows 11 24H2 and later the skin sits right
under the desktop icons, so it stays when Windows rebuilds the wallpaper layer (a game starting, a wallpaper
change, the screen waking up). If the web engine stops or the skin still goes away, Wallplayer starts itself
again within a few seconds (at most 3 times in 10 minutes; after that, quit it from the tray and open it again). What happened is written to %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.tan.wallplayer\wallplayer.log.

SELF-TEST
Press Ctrl + Shift + D (or Settings → General → Self-test) to check the Windows and YouTube features one by one
(volume, Win + D, video colours, captions, video quality). If something fails, send a screenshot of it.

UNINSTALLING
In the tray menu untick "Start with Windows", then "Quit Wallplayer", and delete the Wallplayer folder.
To remove settings and sign-ins too, delete the com.tan.wallplayer folders in %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%.

PRIVACY
No account, no analytics, nothing is sent to the authors. On its own Wallplayer only contacts GitHub once
at start to look for a new version (you can turn it off). Other services (YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify,
lyrics, translation) are used only when you use that feature. Details: PRIVACY.md on GitHub.

LINUX AND MACOS (PREVIEW)
These builds have not been tried on a real Linux PC or Mac yet. Each system has its own zip on the
release page:
- Linux (64-bit): Wallplayer-Linux-x64.zip. Unzip it and run Wallplayer/wallplayer. It needs WebKitGTK 4.1
  (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer; if it does not start: sudo apt install libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0 libayatana-appindicator3-1).
- macOS (Apple silicon and Intel): Wallplayer-macOS.zip. Unzip it and move Wallplayer.app to Applications.
What differs from Windows:
- Linux: Wallplayer becomes the desktop itself (an X11 desktop window, the kind file managers use), below
  every app and on every workspace; "Show desktop" leaves it in place. It covers the desktop icons of
  your file manager, if it shows any, and takes the mouse directly. On a Wayland session it runs through
  XWayland. "Start at login" adds ~/.config/autostart/wallplayer.desktop. Songs started from Wallplayer in other
  apps (Spotify, a browser) are read through MPRIS; the system volume from PipeWire / PulseAudio; the
  ambient light hears the sound through `parec` or `pw-record`.
- macOS: Wallplayer sits on the desktop of every Space, under all apps. With "Hide desktop icons" on it
  covers Finder's icons and takes the mouse; off, it sits under them. "Open at login" adds a
  LaunchAgent (~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.tan.wallplayer.plist). The app is not signed by Apple: the
  first time, right-click Wallplayer.app → Open. Songs you start from Wallplayer in Spotify or Apple
  Music show in the player (macOS asks once whether Wallplayer may control them). The search shortcut is ⇧⌘Space, because
  macOS uses ⌃⌥Space to switch the keyboard language. The ambient light follows its own animation:
  macOS does not let apps hear the system sound without the Screen Recording permission.
- Not on Linux / macOS: "High priority" (a Windows feature).

KNOWN LIMITS
- Some YouTube videos cannot be embedded; Wallplayer tries another one.
- Google may refuse sign-in in an embedded window for some accounts.
- Genius, YouTube and machine translation use unofficial interfaces that may change at any time.
